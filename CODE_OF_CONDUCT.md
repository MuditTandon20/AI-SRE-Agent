# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss ideas and code constructively, welcome questions, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information are unacceptable in project discussions, issues and pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed. They will consider context and aim for fair, proportionate responses. To report a concern, ask a maintainer for a private contact channel without posting sensitive details publicly. Do not retaliate against someone who raises a concern in good faith.
